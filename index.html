The World

The Fortress Awakens

It's been a thousand years since The Fortress vanished from the world. Its Gates, magical portals once used to join nations, remained dormant for centuries, until their powers were no more than legends.

But centuries later, tales of Legendary Weapons, God-like Crystals and Lost Civilizations suddenly came true, when the Fortress Core inexplicably regained its power.

Its Entrances reactivated all around the planet, leading only the bravest into the remains of an underground kingdom frozen in time.

3 vs 3 Multiplayer

Two teams of explorers battle within the ancient fortress in search for the Dungeon Core, a lost source of infinite energy.

Room-Based Map

The 3-lane map is divided in monster-filled chambers! Face enemy explorers, dark creatures and deadly traps on your way to the Core.

RPG Progression & Roguelike Powerups

Don't just master abilities and level up Character Traits! Create a unique build of Elemental Powerups in classic Roguelike fashion to become unstoppable.

Fast Paced Strategy and Combat!

Experience thrilling Teamfights in 15-Minute Matches, with dynamic combat controls, while keeping the Team Strategy and Progression of the MOBA.
